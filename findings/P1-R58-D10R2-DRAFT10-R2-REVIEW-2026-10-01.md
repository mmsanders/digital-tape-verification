## Ask 2: the r2 crash table is exact over the #133 enumeration

`P1-R58-D10R2/r2_table_check.py` imports the #133 `d10_analysis.py` unchanged (SHA-256
`de59a91b83b14b94d3735ae94a05eaf0a99b0ac3871824017ed680255e0411e0`) and the `tests/wp10_backlog_r54`
model.

For every starting destination, it does the following:

1. Plans the run's **path** from raw bytes: template, generation-exhausted fallback (with candidate or
   equal-generation-divergent), residue, or blank.
2. Assigns each injection's **phase**: step-1 first write, step-1 last write, steps 2–3, step-4
   mirror, step-4 primary, or after 4.
3. Selects the r2 §9.5 rows that apply, and requires the classifier's outcome to satisfy one of them:
   unchanged, `INCOMPLETE`, surviving primary, unmountable (`BAD_MAGIC`/`CRC`), or the completed copy.

**Coverage:**

- **One interruption:** all 14 #133 shapes, every write (before, torn 1…511, after) and every flush,
  both modes: **264,626** durable states.
- **Two interruptions:** every distinct one-interruption state (by primary, mirror and A0 header:
  **3,065** starts) as the destination of a re-run. Each re-run is injected at every landed length of
  every superblock write and every flush, both modes: **12,563,940** states. Steps 2–3 write no
  superblock, so their outcome is fixed by the state step 1 leaves; that phase is covered by the
  one-interruption pass.

**Result: 0 states without a permitted row, and 0 outside their row**
(`P1-R58-D10R2/r2_table_check.log`). Every permitted outcome of every row is reached, with one
exception: `TAPE_ERR_CRC` in the rows where no copy retains its magic bytes. Those rows are a torn last
fallback zero, steps 2–3 after fallback or residue zeroing, and blank steps 2–3. That is **not**
over-permission. tapefs §4.1 phase 1 lets an engine return either code whenever neither copy is valid,
and V10-003 adopts that wording deliberately (see V10R2-002).

`tape_format` (§9.6) issues the same superblock-relevant sequence, with only the step numbering
differing, and its r2 rows mirror §9.5 row for row.

## Ask 4: V10R-003 wording

The new WP-06f re-spool clause is exactly rule (b) of the #108-corrected `wp06_closure_r52` oracle
(`check_floor_row`, Verification #108 publication `6b92e5f`):

- **It admits the lawful #108 trace.** Pass 1 writes [5,8) above the live-B floor 5, then commits.
  Pass 2 writes [2,5), which were live B only **before** pass 1 committed; they are freed and
  ≥ `a_high_water` 2 at that moment. That synthetic trace passes the oracle.
- **It rejects writing a chunk live at that moment.** The `second_phase_live` control writes chunk 5
  after the pass-1 commit and is red.
- **It rejects writing below `a_high_water`.** The `second_phase_shape` control moves pass 2 to chunk 1
  and is red ("pass-2 write below a_high_water").
- **Promote keeps its floor.** "Starts phase 1's staging run at `S = free_next` on the allocating
  path" is §9.3.1, and "writes `[0, len)` in phase 2 only after step 4" is §9.3.2. That is rule (a)
  plus rule (c) of the same oracle (`below_floor_unreferenced` red; the lawful phase 2 to [0,3) green).

## New findings

### V10R2-001: minor. The V10R-005 replacement over-reaches (my own suggested wording)

**Text.**

- Acceptance "Format, blank media": "The only permitted 'does not mount' outcomes in the whole suite
  are destinations with no structurally valid superblock: blank, residue (V10-001), or after fallback
  zeroing."
- tapefs §9.6 blank row: "The only unmountable outcomes in this table are destinations with no
  structurally valid superblock — blank, residue, or after fallback zeroing".

**Problem.** The same tables and rows also permit non-mounting outcomes that keep a structurally valid
superblock: `TAPE_ERR_INCOMPLETE` once a template write is durable, `TAPE_ERR_INCONSISTENT` on
equal-generation-divergent, and a surviving copy's own refusal (V8R2-001, e.g. `TAPE_ERR_VERSION`).
Read literally, the acceptance sentence contradicts the Format-reusable row directly above it. The
tapefs sentence is defensible only if "unmountable" means the table's own BAD_MAGIC/CRC label. The
wording follows my #133 V10R-005 suggestion, which had the same flaw.

**Fix.** "The only outcomes with **no structurally valid superblock** (`TAPE_ERR_BAD_MAGIC` or
`TAPE_ERR_CRC`) are blank, residue, or after fallback zeroing. Every other non-mounting outcome
(`TAPE_ERR_INCOMPLETE`, `TAPE_ERR_INCONSISTENT`, a surviving copy's own refusal) is listed in its row."
Use the same scoping in tapefs §9.6.

### V10R2-002: info. `TAPE_ERR_CRC` is permitted where it cannot arise under a magic-first reading

Ask 2 shows `CRC` never arises where no copy keeps its magic. The rows still permit it because §4.1
phase 1 leaves the choice open, consistent with V10-003 and the #109 ruling. No change is requested.
If PM wants exactness, §4.1 could state "`BAD_MAGIC` when neither copy has the magic; otherwise
`CRC`", but that is a §4.1 behaviour statement inside the freeze, and not needed for issuance.
