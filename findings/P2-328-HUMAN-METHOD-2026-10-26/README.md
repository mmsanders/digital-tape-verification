## Review of proposed collector and source crosswalk

Software correctly discloses that564 does not persist the invocation receipt,
report or published pair after the worker's immediate owned-job cleanup. Its
report/pair/stdout retention limits justify HOLDING those facts before collection.
Do not add live report opens/hardlinks, delaycleanup, replace app/child or run CLI.

But its promised lossless child boundary is not established:

1. Microsoft documents non-guaranteed delivery of ordinary job completion-port
   messages and warns of process-ID reuse/association races. Opening a child handle
   only after NEW_PROCESS cannot guarantee observing every short child. The app's
   own temporary handle is not a guarantee the proposed collector acquires it in time.
   Assigning the app first and only later associating the port/printing READY is not
   an established uninterrupted receipt boundary. These are documentation findings,
   not a trial or measured Product defect.
2. FileSystemWatcher can overflow and lose names. Capturing overflow helps report
   incompleteness; names alone do not authenticate content, nonce records, argv,
   image/parent identity or exit. End-of-run aggregate process totals do not localize
   complete per-gesture negative intervals or resolve missed attribution.
3. Accepted source maps genuine production events through main.rs/app.mjs to
   Bridge::drop_paths/admission, serial Work, jobdir::create, child run and cleanup.
   It shows WHAT this exact code does. A job directory/name or UI change alone does
   not prove that this particular real gesture caused IPC. A parent matching the app
   alone does not exclude injected invocation through the app itself.

Primary bases, retrieved26October2026:
[Microsoft completion-port job documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/winnt/ns-winnt-jobobject_associate_completion_port),
[Microsoft FileSystemWatcher buffer documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/system.io.filesystemwatcher.internalbuffersize?view=netframework-4.8.1).
These support limits on this proposal; no assertion is made that the APIs never
work. We have NOT performed a new job-object/provider/watcher experiment.

**event_ipc_ref/worker_ref judgment:** no literal packet dump is inherently required.
An accepted source causal map PLUS an independently adequate current chain might
be sufficient in principle. THIS proposal lacks complete current attribution,
argv/report/pair originals and robust boundary, so those fields cannot be marked
observed. Current decision is HELD, not a demand to install packet-capture tools.
No collector is qualified or authorized here; do not write/run it to discover its
limits. Future broader qualification requires fresh scoped admission, not a retry
