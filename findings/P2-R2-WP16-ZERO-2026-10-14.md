## P2V16-001 — a legal silent source can require an unloadable zero-frame WAV

INGEST-1 §2 admits PCM16 mono at192000Hz with positive decoded/effective frame count.
§3 makes an entirely zero effective source exact digital silence, with no gain/dither.
§4's mandatory nearest/half-up count for N=1 is:

`floor((1*44100 + 96000)/192000) = 0`.

The source is not a zero-frame input and no trim made its effective count zero, so the
existing zero-frame input/effective-interval refusals do not cover it. The digital-silence
exception also removes the ordinary valid-peak requirement for this source. With one
folder there is no inserted gap, so the prescribed output is a canonical44-byte WAV
with no PCM. §5 does not explicitly forbid such a canonical header. Retained WP16 I2
requires load-compatible output; that output cannot be loaded by today's accepted CLI.

| Issued arithmetic at192000Hz | Source/effective frames | Prescribed output frames | Tested shipping CLI load |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Silent source | 1 | 0 | exit1, `TAPE_ERR_INVALID_ARG` in promote |
| Silent source | 2 | 0 | exit1, same finding |
| Neighbouring positive output | 3 | 1 | exit0; verifyOK/0 |

The zero-output boundary also occurs at N=1/r96000 and N=1/r176400. At N=1/r88200 the
half-up count is1, showing why a blanket short-source refusal would be unnecessarily broad.
For a nonzero source producing no samples, §3's missing-valid-peak refusal already helps;
the all-zero case bypasses that protection and needs an explicit ruling.

## PM decision and next owner

**Recommend one bounded contract amendment:** after valid decode/trim/count and before
output, refuse any source with computed output_frames=0, including digital silence;
exit2, name the source and explain that it is too short to produce a44.1kHz frame.
Keep positive short/unmeasurable sources with output_frames≥1 accepted under the issued
exceptions. This preserves exact rounding, concatenation, gap arithmetic, and the
existing shipping engine/CLI; Verification then includes the named refusal and its
N=3 positive neighbour as a causal pair.

Alternatives: permit an empty final output while explicitly removing its load-compatibility
claim (changes the product promise), or force at least one output frame (changes the
issued count/no-extra-frame rule). Neither is silently selected by Verification.

Return directly to PM384 and partner Software429, with no implementation/oracle
relaxation. Keep #169 open: only the affected zero-output assertions await issuance,
and unaffected authoring can resume after priority #146 review. No full Stage1 handoff
or Stage2 PASS is claimed. WP14 physicalA6, WP16 real-card/WP15 and every standing
hardware/media/atomicity/<30s/write/async/recording/frozen-spec hold remain.
