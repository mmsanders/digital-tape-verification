## Operator checker

[Check-WP14Trial.ps1](Check-WP14Trial.ps1) reads **only saved local trial files**. It does not open a physical device, call tapectl, provision, repair, change metadata or write a report. Green results license only the already-published narrow predicate: exact quiet/quiet baseline/recovery with verifyOK and consistent input/target/command/exit/timing records. New/mixed/staged/error outcomes remain independent-review cases; the checker does not invent a general crash oracle or final ten-trial acceptance.

Download the checker into the existing tapetest folder. Two times to use it:

1. **After instruction set4**, check the trial you just completed. Green means keep that folder, then begin a new numbered trial from fresh5400s provision. It does not mean load C60 again on this folder/card state.
2. **After fresh provision, quiet baseline and before-checks**, but before instruction set3, check the new folder with BeforeLoad. Green means begin this trial's C60 load and physical pull. A folder that already contains a load attempt is rejected for this stage.

```powershell
# Completed trial02, after set4:
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Check-WP14Trial.ps1 -TrialFolder .\pull-win-02 -Stage AfterPull

# New trial03, after its fresh provision + quiet + before checks, before C60:
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Check-WP14Trial.ps1 -TrialFolder .\pull-win-03 -Stage BeforeLoad
```

The invocation's execution-policy setting is limited to that checker process; no machine policy setting is changed. It permits the explicitly requested downloaded read-only checker to run. No disk-safety policy is bypassed. The checker itself does not need Administrator privileges; actual original tapectl procedure still does.
