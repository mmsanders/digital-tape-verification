## P2READ-002 — precise blocker and recommendation to PM

The issued read-only optimization changes legal read callback grouping and removes
rereads; the retained raw regeneration baselines encode the previous read algorithm.
The independent comparison shows no changed recorded behavior/media/PCM/write/flush
facts, but mandatory exact-byte regeneration still fails. **Do not waive it or
silently overwrite accepted evidence.** No engine correction is demonstrated by
this delta; forcing the old read trace would defeat the issued optimization.

Recommend an explicit, narrowly scoped PM retained-transcript binding disposition:

1. Preserve the old accepted streams, hashes, original engine pins and their offline
   replay permanently. Issue a distinct read-optimized transcript epoch for only
   these six independently audited suites on the exact engine tree above.
2. Bind fresh raw transcripts/hashes to their truthful executed head/build/adapter
   and the new epoch. Keep exact regeneration against that issued baseline;
   do not globally strip events, ignore reads or select a baseline from moving main.
3. Preserve all existing behavior/write/persistence/PCM oracles, exact goldens and
   negative controls. Require old-vs-new non-event and ordered write/flush identity
   checks for the issued transition and retain READ-1 actual read/budget/work gates.
4. Re-establish a green benign mutation baseline plus genuine caught mutation/gate
   controls and required retained-regression CI on the final head. Independently
   dispose any changed verifier/binding bytes and new exact head before integration.
