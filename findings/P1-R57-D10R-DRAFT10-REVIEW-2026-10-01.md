## Method: exhaustive enumeration, not argument

`P1-R57-D10R/d10_analysis.py` implements the tapefs §9.5 step order with the raw-destination
classification of **either** DRAFT-9 (no valid copy → blank) **or** DRAFT-10 (blank only if both blocks
are all zero, otherwise residue: zero mirror, flush, zero primary, flush). Everything else uses the
verifier model of `tests/wp10_backlog_r54` (Verification #116, PR #117, commit `dea9b521…`):
transactions, the tapefs §8.1 durability model, and the §4.1/§4.2/§5.2/§5.3 mount classifier, which
was cross-checked there against the accepted R29-B step-1 writes on all seven R29-B shapes.

**Shapes (14):**

- the seven R29-B raw-admission shapes (`healthy_pair`, `mirror_only`, `generation_zero`, `v2_only`,
  `equal_divergent`, `exhaustion_candidate`, `exhaustion_equal_divergent`);
- blank;
- two **mirror-candidate exhaustion** shapes that R29-B does not have (`exhaustion_mirror_only`:
  primary zero, mirror valid at `0xFFFFFFFD`; `exhaustion_mirror_candidate`: mirror at `0xFFFFFFFD`
  over a primary at `0xFFFFFFFC`);
- four residue shapes: torn primary beside zero, torn mirror beside zero, foreign data in both blocks,
  and all-`0xFF` (erased flash).

**One interruption:** every write (before, torn 1…511, after) and every flush, in both durability
modes. Flush-required pending writes may or may not have landed.

**Two interruptions:** from every distinct first-crash durable state, re-run `tape_dup`. That re-run is
planned from the crashed bytes under the same rule, and is interrupted at every landed length of
**every superblock write** it issues (residue zeros, template or fallback writes, fresh mirror, fresh
primary), in both modes.

**Violation:** a remount that succeeds under a non-fresh identity while the copied A0 index is durable
(silent substitution), or a successful mount of a destination that had no valid superblock.

| DRAFT-9 | 0 | 12,461,618 | **528**: 132 in each of `exhaustion_candidate`, `exhaustion_equal_divergent`, `exhaustion_mirror_only`, `exhaustion_mirror_candidate` |
| **DRAFT-10** | **0** | 16,890,042 | **0** on all 14 shapes |

Per-shape logs are `P1-R57-D10R/two_draft9.log` and `two_draft10.log`.

`d9_pairs.py` pins the DRAFT-9 cells exactly:

- In each of the four affected shapes and each mode, **85** (L1, L2) pairs resurrect the previous
  superblock. L1 is the landed length of the fallback's **last** zero (1…12). L2 is the landed length
  of the fresh write to the same copy (≤ 12, the 12 bytes of magic and version the two superblocks
  share).
- Pairs with L2 < L1 exist because bytes 6, 9, 10 and 11 of the old superblock are already zero.
- The copy is the **primary** for R29-B's two exhaustion shapes, and the **mirror** for the two
  mirror-candidate shapes (`d9_pairs.log`).

## Ask 1: V10-001 closure

- **Hazard closed.** There are zero two-interruption violations under DRAFT-10 on every shape above,
  including both exhaustion shapes, equal-generation-divergent, mirror-only, generation 0,
  `version_major` 2, foreign data, residue, and the two mirror-candidate exhaustion shapes. No
  injection mounts under the previous UUID or label over the copied indices.
- **Tear safety holds.** The magic's first byte is `0x54`, so any landed prefix of a zero write breaks
  structural validity, and a write landing nothing changes nothing. In the enumeration, every
  residue-zeroing state remounts unmountable (`TAPE_ERR_BAD_MAGIC`, or `TAPE_ERR_CRC` where the
  classifier admits it) and re-classifies as residue.
- **Re-run holds.** From every residue state the re-run zeroes and then reaches the completed copy.
- **Identity boundary (V8C-002).** After residue zeroing no higher-generation copy survives, so the
  generation-1 identity commit is the only valid generation.
- **Write-through.** Both modes are enumerated.
- **Blank path.** An all-zero destination is planned with **no** step-1 write.
- **Permitted sets.** The new and changed §9.5/§9.6 rows are exact for every state the enumeration
  reaches, **except** one state that has no row (**V10R-001**).

## Ask 3: V10-002 … V10-005

- **V10-002: exact.**
  - Every tapefs §9.3.3 RESUME row requires a single-entry live Side B, leaving 4,095 of
    `TAPE_MAX_ENTRIES` free. `tape_arm`'s `INDEX_FULL` (engine-api §7, "insufficient for the
    requested mode") is therefore unreachable on stage-1 media.
  - A zero-entry Side B is a valid, non-degraded index that matches no row, so mount refuses it with
    `TAPE_ERR_INCONSISTENT` first.
  - Both refusals stay asserted on stage-0 media: `record_draft8` WP09-INDEX-FULL (accepted, #132)
    and `respool_draft8` WP12-EMPTY.
- **V10-003: matches tapefs §4.1 phase 1** ("Neither valid → `TAPE_ERR_BAD_MAGIC` or `TAPE_ERR_CRC`")
  and the #109 PM ruling. The "both fallback zeros durable → `BAD_MAGIC`" text is correctly left
  `BAD_MAGIC`-only, because all-zero blocks carry no magic.
- **V10-004: the promote floor is not weakened.** `tapefs` §9.3.1 `S = free_next` is kept. The
  re-spool clause is ambiguous in a way that reintroduces the #108 defect; see **V10R-003**.
- **V10-005: exact.** engine-api §10 marks `respool` and `promote` `B` from Playing, and `set_rate`
  is `B` in every in-progress row. It matches the #104 PM finding.

## Ask 4: Testability

The new acceptance WP-10 "Residue destinations" paragraph is mechanically testable once V10R-001,
V10R-002 and V10R-006 are applied.

**What replaces the 48 V-R54-03 cells.** Not 48 cells, and not only the primary.

- The DRAFT-9 hazard was **85 cells per mode** in each exhaustion shape: 340 over R29-B's two
  exhaustion shapes (**V10R-004**), plus 340 through the mirror in the two mirror-candidate shapes
  (**V10R-002**).
- The republished row should enumerate exhaustively, not replay a fixed list. For each of the four
  exhaustion shapes (two R29-B, two mirror-candidate), each mode, and each L1 in 1…511 (the residue the
  torn last zero leaves):
  - every residue-zeroing boundary of the re-run (2 writes × 513 + 2 flushes);
  - every landed length of **both** fresh superblock writes (2 × 513 + 2 flushes).
- Add residue shapes (b) on both copies and (c).
- Format equivalents use the same structure.

## Findings

### V10R-001: major. The torn **second** fallback zero on equal-generation-divergent has no crash-table row

**Text.** New tapefs §9.5 row (and §9.6 equivalent): "Step 1's boundary fallback on a shape **with a §4.1
candidate**: non-selectable copy zeroed, **candidate zero tore** → Unmountable …". Acceptance residue
paragraph (a): "the residue an exhaustion-shape fallback leaves when its **candidate** zero tears …, on
**both exhaustion shapes**".

**Problem.**

- `exhaustion_equal_divergent` has **no** §4.1 candidate. Its fallback zeroes the mirror, then the
  primary.
- The state "mirror zero durable, primary zero torn (1…511)" is reached in the enumeration and is
  unmountable. It is neither "primary not zeroed" (the V8R2-001 row) nor "both zeroed" (`BAD_MAGIC`),
  and the new row excludes it by its own condition.
- So no row of either table covers it, yet acceptance (a) mandates exactly that injection on this shape.

**Fix.**

- Generalise the row to "Step 1's boundary fallback: first zero durable, **second (last) zero tore** —
  any shape, including equal-generation-divergent → Unmountable (`TAPE_ERR_BAD_MAGIC` or
  `TAPE_ERR_CRC`); the destination is residue; a re-run zeroes it first".
- In acceptance (a), say "its **last** zero tears".

### V10R-002: major. The residue closure tears only the fresh **primary**; resurrection can go through the **mirror**

**Text.** Acceptance: "re-run and inject a torn write at every landed length of the **fresh primary**";
(a) lists "both exhaustion shapes"; (b) "a torn or CRC-broken copy beside an all-zero copy".

**Problem.**

- The fallback zeroes the selected candidate last. When the candidate is the **mirror** (the V6-008
  mirror-only shape at `sb_generation ≥ 0xFFFFFFFD`, or a current mirror over a lower primary), the
  torn last zero leaves the residue in the **mirror**.
- Under DRAFT-9 those two shapes resurrect through a torn **fresh mirror** write: 85 cells per mode
  each (`d9_pairs.log`).
- Neither shape is in (a), which names R29-B's two primary-candidate shapes, and the closure never
  tears the fresh mirror.
- An engine that zeroes residue only in the primary, or classifies a mirror-side residue as blank,
  would pass the paragraph as written.

**Fix.**

- Add the two mirror-candidate exhaustion shapes to (a).
- State (b) for **each** copy.
- Make the closure "every landed length of **each** fresh superblock write (mirror and primary)".

### V10R-003: major. V10-004 "never writes into Side B's live chunks" is ambiguous and can reintroduce #108

**Text.** WP-06f: "a `respool` issued from it **never writes into Side B's live chunks** and never below
`a_high_water` (`tapefs` §9.4)".

**Problem.**

- tapefs §9.4 (b) is disjointness from the live set of either side **at that moment**. Pass 2 lawfully
  lands on chunks pass 1 freed, which were Side B's live chunks **before** the operation. That is the
  #108 PM ruling, and the dense-fixture lawful trace pass 1 [5,8) → pass 2 [2,5) is exactly this.
- Read against the pre-operation live set, the clause fails every conforming engine whose pass 2 runs.

**Fix.**

- Say "never writes a chunk that is live on either side **at the time of that write** (invariant 10),
  and never below `a_high_water` (§9.4 (a))".
- For promote, say "phase 1's staging run starts at `S = free_next` on the allocating path (§9.3.1);
  phase 2 writes `[0, len)` only after step 4 (§9.3.2)". That keeps the floor without inviting the
  other half of the #108 misreading.

### V10R-004: minor. The "48 DRAFT-9 cells" is a Verification undercount; don't cite a count

**Text.** Acceptance: "This replaces the 48 DRAFT-9 cells Verification recorded as V-R54-03."

**Problem.**

- V-R54-03 (Verification #118, `wp10_final_r54`) counted 12 landed lengths × 2 modes × 2 shapes, with
  the first tear fixed at 1 byte.
- The exhaustive enumeration finds 85 (L1, L2) cells per mode per shape: 340 over the same two shapes,
  and 680 including the mirror-candidate shapes.
- This is a correction to Verification's own prior count. It is outside the changed text, but the
  changed text cites it.

**Fix.** Replace the sentence with a property reference ("This replaces the DRAFT-9 V-R54-03
cells") and leave the census to the republished row.

### V10R-005: minor. "The single permitted does-not-mount case" is no longer true

**Text.**

- Acceptance "Format, blank media": "This is the single permitted 'does not mount' case in the whole
  suite."
- tapefs §9.6 blank row: "This is the one case where 'remount succeeds' cannot hold".

**Problem.** DRAFT-10 permits unmountable outcomes on residue zeroing and on a torn last fallback zero,
and the both-zeroed fallback was already unmountable in DRAFT-9.

**Fix.** Reword to "the only unmountable outcomes are destinations with no structurally valid
superblock: blank, residue, or after fallback zeroing".

### V10R-006: minor. The residue paragraph's absolute identity assertion needs a scope for shape (a)

**Text.** "**No injection may yield a cartridge that mounts under the destination's previous UUID or
label**".

**Problem.** Shape (a)'s residue is produced by a fallback run whose earlier boundaries legitimately
mount the old cartridge: the "old cartridge unchanged" row, and V8R2-001's surviving primary.

**Fix.** State that (a)'s residue is the precondition. Equivalently: from the torn last zero onward,
and over copied indices, no injection mounts under the previous identity. Boundaries before that are
governed by the existing rows.

### V10R-007: minor (informational). Erased (`0xFF`) cards are residue, not blank

**Problem.** "Blank only if both blocks are entirely zero" means an erased card that reads `0xFF`
takes the residue path: two writes and two flushes, then residue rows instead of blank rows. Outcomes
are compatible (unmountable until step 4; verified on `residue_ff_erased`).

**Fix.** Optionally state it, so tests and firmware don't treat all-`0xFF` as blank.

### V10R-008: minor (editorial). Residue and blank rows overlap

**Problem.** The rows "Residue: inside **or after** step 1's residue zeroing, before step 4" and
"Blank (… or after residue zeroing), before step 4's mirror write completes" overlap from the end of
residue zeroing to step 4. Both say unmountable, so they are consistent.

**Fix.** Make the residue row "inside step 1's residue zeroing" and let the blank rows take over after
it.
